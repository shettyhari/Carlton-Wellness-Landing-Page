FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Capital Contribution?

The Capital Contribution is ₹1 Cr for a Tier 1 hotel, mall or airport location and ₹75 Lacs for a Tier 2 gated community location.

How is the Capital Contribution structured?

10% of the Capital Contribution is the brand fee and 90% is a refundable security deposit, under a 5-year agreement. Terms and conditions apply.

What is the Monthly Minimum Payout Protection?

Where contractually applicable, the Business Partner receives a Monthly Minimum Payout Protection of ₹2L per month on a ₹1 Cr Tier 1 centre (₹24L over 12 months) and ₹1.5L per month on a ₹75 Lacs Tier 2 centre (₹18L over 12 months), or 20% of total centre revenue, as set out in the franchise agreement. Program-based visits and high customer lifetime value support stable wellness economics.

Who runs the centre?

Carlton follows a FOCO model: you own the centre and Carlton operates it. SOPs, staffing and therapy design are handled centrally, along with training, audits, procurement, marketing and CCTV and ERP access.

What happens after year 5?

After year 5 the centre can transition from FOCO to FOFO. You take full operational control, retain 90–95% of the margin and pay a 5–10% royalty to Carlton.
